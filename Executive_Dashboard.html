Why Bayesian Networks over Black Boxes?

Black-Box Models (Traditional)

  • High predictive accuracy but bound to independent & identical distributions (IID).
  • Highly susceptible to data drift without warning.
  • Opaque extrapolability. Fails when underlying data structures inevitably shift.
  • Cannot answer "What-If" structural causality questions.

Bayesian Network DAG (Our Architecture)

  • Robust Extrapolability: Maps exact structural invariant relationships. It relies on the causal mechanism, not mere correlation.
  • Mathematically protected against 0-probability edge cases using Dirichlet Pior (BDeu) Smoothing.
  • Enables Causal Do-Calculus: "If we force an authentication check (V4), how much does fraud probability drop?"
